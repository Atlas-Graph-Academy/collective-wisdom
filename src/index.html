Half a million points, far enough away to look like light. Aim at any part of it and keep going until it stops looking like light.

Act one

Dust.

Close enough, the light was never light — it was this. A cloud of grains, each shaking on its own. No two the same. Nothing touching.

Act two

One is struck by light.

It passes it on. Grain by grain the field falls into a single rhythm, and where two beat together a line jumps between them. The first connection.

Act three

The connections grow a body.

Nothing was added. The same grains, the same lines, following one rule long enough to become a form.

Act four

The body grows a mind.

Every line is now a synapse. Move the mouse over it, and use the wheel to get close enough to see one.
